AI for Inclusive MSME Growth Platform
Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) are the economic backbone of
the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), accounting for over 97% of
all business establishments and providing 85% of the region’s employment.
Despite their critical role, MSMEs contribute disproportionately less to the
region's GDP (approximately 45%) and export value (18%), highlighting a
significant productivity and market access gap. This disparity prevents true
inclusive economic growth, as the benefits of the digital economy remain
concentrated among larger corporations.
The primary constraints facing MSMEs include limited access to formal
financing, lack of digital capabilities, and inability to navigate complex crossborder
trade regulations. Many traditional businesses remain offline or
unbanked, leaving them invisible to global markets and formal credit systems.
Providing these enterprises with AI technology is deemed crucial to unlocking
their potential. An AI-driven ecosystem can democratize access to enterprisegrade
tools such as alternative credit scoring, automated supply chain
management, and predictive market analytics enabling MSMEs to thrive, scale
regionally, and contribute significantly to the region's economic resilience.
